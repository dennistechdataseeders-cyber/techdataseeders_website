What is web scraping and how does it work?+

Web scraping is the automated process of extracting structured data from websites and apps. Our scrapers navigate websites, extract needed data, clean and structure it, then deliver it in your preferred format (CSV, JSON, API) on a defined schedule.

Is the data extracted legally and ethically?+

Techdataseeders only extracts publicly available information. We do not scrape personal or identity-related data, and all operations comply with applicable data laws and website terms of service.

What industries do you serve?+

We serve 12+ industries including E-commerce, Real Estate, Finance, Travel, Food Delivery, Healthcare, Sports, and more. Our solutions are tailored to each industry's specific data needs.

How quickly can you deliver data after onboarding?+

For standard projects, first delivery is within 3–7 business days after requirement finalization. Complex multi-source enterprise projects may take 2–3 weeks for full setup and testing.

What formats can data be delivered in?+

We deliver in CSV, JSON, XML, Excel, SQL, Google Sheets, or directly via REST API. We work with whatever format your existing systems require.

How is data accuracy maintained over time?+

We maintain 95%+ accuracy through automated quality checks, anomaly detection, and human review for critical datasets. Our scrapers self-heal when website structures change, with 24/7 monitoring ensuring continuity.
