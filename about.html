About Us

We Turn the Open Web Into Decision-Ready Data

TechDataSeeders is a data-engineering team helping brands extract, structure, and act on web data at scale — accurately, ethically, and on time.

Our Story

Built by Engineers Who Were
Tired of Dirty Data

TechDataSeeders started with a simple frustration: businesses everywhere were drowning in web data they couldn't trust. Scrapers broke overnight, records arrived half-empty, and teams burned hours cleaning spreadsheets instead of making decisions. We believed there was a better way — one built on solid engineering rather than brittle scripts.

Today we operate distributed, fault-tolerant pipelines that deliver more than ten million clean, validated records every day to clients across seven industries. From global enterprises to fast-moving startups, our partners rely on us for the one thing that makes data valuable: trust. We handle the crawling, the anti-bot defenses, the validation, and the delivery — so our clients can focus on insight, not infrastructure.
