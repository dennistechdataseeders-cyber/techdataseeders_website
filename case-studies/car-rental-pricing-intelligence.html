Client Overview

The client is well known Europe based car rental company operating across multiple countries. They offer a wide range of vehicles of various categories, from economy cars to luxury SUVs. As competition growing in global and local markets, they required a pricing intelligence solution and optimize their offerings by keeping profit margin intact.

Client Requirements

The client come up to the Dataseeders with the following requirements:

  • Monitor real-time car rental prices from key competitors across different geographies.
  • Extract complete rental details, including car types, fuel policies, pickup/drop locations, and extra charges.
  • Capture terms and conditions like mileage limits, insurance policies, deposits, and cancellation rules.
  • Get structured data on weekly basis for integration into their internal pricing analysis tool.
  • Use competitor insights to adjust their rental prices strategically while protecting profit margins.
  • Grab attention of price sensitive consumers by offering better deals.

Challenges

The client faced several operational and strategic roadblocks:

  • Regional Pricing Discrepancies: Competing in diverse countries with variable pricing norms made manual tracking incompetent.
  • Dynamic Rate Changes: Competitors alter pricing and offers frequently that makes static research undependable.
  • Complex Rental Terms: Extracting nuanced terms & conditions like insurance coverage or location-based fees was time taking and error prone.
  • Data Standardization Issues: Comparing listings from different platforms was difficult due to uneven data formats.
  • Absence of Integration: The client needed data that could integrated directly into their existing system and BI tools.

Dataseeders Solutions

DataSeeders designed a customized weekly data scraping and intelligence solution with seamless integration abilities.

Real-Time Competitor Monitoring

Tracked rental prices across leading local and global platforms by location, car type, and rental duration.

Complete Rental Info Extraction

Collected organized data on vehicle specifications, availability, taxes, fees, insurance, mileage, and more.

Terms & Conditions Parsing

Used progressive NLP to extract and standardize complex rental terms for accurate analysis.

Weekly Data Delivery

Delivered data in XML files on a as per schedule, optimized for direct ingestion into their pricing system.

Custom Dashboard

Provided optional BI dashboards to visualize price changes, competitor trends, and category-level benchmarks.

Benefits to the Client

  • Enabled price benchmarking across 6 countries and over 150 car categories.
  • Better competitiveness by adjusting pricing to outrank key rivals without affecting margins.
  • Reduced manual tracking hours by 90%, freeing up operational resources to focus on other important strategies.
  • Improved pricing decision precision with constant weekly insights.
  • Boosted new customer bookings by 24% within the first quarter of implementation.
  • Strategic clarity in running promotions, flash sales, and bundle offers based on data insights.

Conclusion

With our weekly real-time data scraping solution, the client gained a influential competitive edge in the global car rental market. Our solution not only improved pricing accuracy and quickness, but also empowered smarter margin control which helping them to win more bookings while staying profitable in every region they served.
