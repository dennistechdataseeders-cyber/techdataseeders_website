Client Overview

Our client is a swiftly rising multi-category eCommerce platform serving millions of customers across Southeast Asia. Having wide range catalog of electronics, fashion, home goods, and more, the client aims to offer competitive pricing, seamless shopping experiences, and vendor transparency.

Client Requirements

To sustain a competitive edge in an increasingly crowded marketplace, the client contacted us with the following requirement:

  • A real-time pricing intelligence system to track and analyze competitor prices across thousands of SKUs.
  • Insights into dynamic pricing trends across categories and brands.
  • Get custom alerts for price drops or Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) policy violation.
  • Benchmarking tools to assess vendor pricing and regulate margins accordingly.

Challenges

Before partnering with DataSeeders, the client confronted numerous functioning blockages:

  • Manual competitor tracking across platforms was time-consuming and prone to errors.
  • Inconsistent product pricing increased cart abandonment rate and reduced consumer trust.
  • Due to delay in receiving price updates, client lost opportunities to more agile competitors.
  • Lack of centralized intelligence affect decisions making process across divisions (pricing, category, procurement).

Dataseeders Solutions

To accomplish the client's pricing goals, we implemented a custom Real-Time Pricing Intelligence Platform:

Automated Price Monitoring

Scraped competitor prices across 17+ marketplaces and brand platforms every 30 minutes.

Dynamic Dashboards

Delivered real-time visualizations for category managers to track pricing trends and outliers.

MAP Violation Alerts

Recognized and flagged sellers violating pricing rules within minutes of exposure.

AI Powered Insights

Applied ML models to estimate optimal pricing based on historical data, seasonality, and competitor movements.

API Integration

Enabled real time synchronization with the internal pricing system for automated repricing and alerts.

Benefits to the Client

  • 23% Increase in Buy Box Wins across top-selling categories by making pricing competitive.
  • Reduced Pricing Errors by 85% through automation process and validation layers.
  • 12% Upsurge in Gross Margin by finding overpriced and underpriced SKUs.
  • Saved 190+ man hours/month by removing manual monitoring.
  • Enhanced Vendor Compliance through transparent reporting and timely alerts.
  • Better Conversion Rates due to consistent and optimized pricing shown to end users.

Conclusion

DataSeeders' real-time pricing intelligence solution allowed the client to stay alert, competitive, and customer focused in a dynamic marketplace environment. By transforming how they track, analyze, and react to market pricing, the client was able to find new growth opportunities and enhanced profitability at scale.
