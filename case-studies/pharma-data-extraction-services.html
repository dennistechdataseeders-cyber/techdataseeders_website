Client Overview

The client is a pharmaceutical manufacturer and distributor operational across multiple regions in the USA, including B2B and D2C channels. Their product range embraces prescription drugs, OTC products, and wellness supplements. They sell products via their own platform, third party marketplaces, and local pharmacy chains.

With increasing product lines and market presence, the client was struggling to apply effective decision-making process for product pricing, inventory management, demand planning, and monitoring competitive positions.

Client Requirements

The client approached us with the goal to:

  • Extract real-time pricing and stock availability from competitor platforms and pharma marketplaces.
  • Monitor marketing tactics of combating brands and analyze seasonal trends.
  • Track product listings, packaging types, dosage info, and regulatory data across platforms.
  • Automate market research and regulatory content updates to save time and ensure compliance.
  • Get clean, structured data in a excel format or via APIs for internal use.

Challenges

  • Competitor data was outspread across various platforms (eCommerce, government registries, B2B portals), and behind login, authentication or with multifaceted layouts.
  • Recurrent changes in website structure and HTML made manual or static scraping ineffective.
  • Uneven data formats led to difficulty in integrating data insights into their existing analytics system.
  • Manual competitor monitoring and product matching process was time consuming and unreliable.
  • Client needs region specific data insights to make effective pricing and stock management decisions.

Dataseeders Solutions

We provided a custom data extraction solution that is aligned with the client's business goals:

Customized Data Scraping Pipelines

Built scalable scripts to extract product pricing, availability, packaging info, and SKUs across competitors, marketplaces, and pharma platforms.

Login and Location Based Data Extraction

Secure login-based scraping with regional aiming to fetch precise location specific insights.

Structured and Cleaned Data Delivery

Processed and regularized data into consistent formats, delivered via secure API and downloadable files.

Automated Monitoring System

We arranged scheduled data collection process to monitor changes in pricing, promotions, stock availability, and new product launches.

Regulatory and Product Data Intelligence

Scraped and classified public domain regulatory documents to help them stay updated with compliance norms.

Benefits to the Client

  • Manual research time reduced by 90% that allow internal teams to focus on strategic work.
  • Client is now able to make quick pricing decisions that is supported by real-time competitor monitoring across regions.
  • Improved stock management, by tracking out-of-stock patterns and seasonal demand.
  • Client can now build effective promotional campaign with aid of timely insights on discounts, combo offers, and bundling trends.
  • Enhanced cost-effectiveness, by identifying underpriced or overstocked SKUs and adjusting them competently.

Conclusion

With our tailored data extraction services, the client transmuted their pharma business into a data-centric operation. From dynamic pricing to proficient stock management, our solution directly contributed to streamlining their internal processes and driving profitability at scale.
