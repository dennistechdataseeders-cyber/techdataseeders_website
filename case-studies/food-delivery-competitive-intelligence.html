Client Overview

The client is one of the leading food delivery aggregators in a rapidly growing market, serving millions of customers daily through their mobile app and web platform. Their marketplace includes thousands of restaurant partners and cloud kitchens. With increasing competition and consumer demand shifts, they were looking to gain deeper market insights, improve discoverability, and increase customer engagement through data-driven decisions.

Client Requirements

The client approached us with the following goals to achieve:

  • Track and analyze competitor menus, pricing strategies, and promotions across diverse regions.
  • Understand consumer behavior trends, review sentiments, and food preferences by area and cuisine.
  • Gain visibility into restaurant partner performance, availability, ratings, and popular food items.
  • Benchmark their brand visibility and discoverability against competitor listings.
  • Use data intelligence to optimize marketing, boost customer acquisition, and enhance app engagement.

Challenges

  • Disorganized and scattered data across multiple competitor apps and platforms made market intelligence difficult.
  • Competitor menu, price, and offer information changed frequently and dynamically, making manual monitoring unsustainable.
  • Lack of visibility into real-time customer reviews, feedback trends, and service ratings.
  • Difficulty in tracking regional-level competition, especially in high-growth tier-2 and tier-3 cities.
  • Inability to link external competitor data with internal marketing and product performance for actionable insights.

Dataseeders Solutions

We delivered a full-scale data extraction and analytics solution, designed to help the client outperform competitors and better serve consumers:

Real-Time Competitive Menu and Price Monitoring

Scraped competitor food delivery platforms to extract live pricing, offers, combos, menu availability, and delivery fees segmented by region and cuisine.

Customer Review & Sentiment Analysis Engine

Extracted and analyzed review content and star ratings across platforms to uncover trending dishes, complaints, service gaps, and brand perception insights.

Restaurant Partner Intelligence Dashboard

Built custom dashboards to track top-rated restaurants, delivery time comparisons, hygiene ratings, and service performance of competitors.

Brand Visibility & Search Ranking Tracker

Monitored app visibility and keyword ranking in food categories to benchmark how the client's listings performed against rivals in different cities.

Actionable Insights Reports with Recommendations

Delivered weekly and monthly reports with visual analytics, trend forecasting, and optimization tips for pricing, promotions, and campaigns.

Benefits to the Client

  • Improved brand visibility on key food and cuisine search results through data-informed keyword optimization and listing strategy.
  • Outranked competitors in multiple regions by matching offers, reducing delivery delays, and introducing popular dishes found via insights.
  • Expanded consumer base by 22% in underpenetrated regions by launching hyper-targeted campaigns based on local preferences.
  • Enhanced app engagement and order volume, driven by better restaurant recommendations and personalized offers derived from customer behavior insights.
  • Achieved data-driven decision making across marketing, operations, and partner strategy teams using centralized insights.

Conclusion

With the right combination of data extraction, competitive analytics, and customer behavior insights, our client was able to leap ahead of the competition, gain visibility in crowded markets, and drive sustainable growth in user base and profitability.
