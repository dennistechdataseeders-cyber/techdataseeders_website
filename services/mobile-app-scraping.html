Mobile App Data Extraction

What is Mobile App Scraping?

Mobile App Scraping is the process of collecting structured information from mobile applications and app stores. Depending on the target app and the type of data required, collection can involve app store sources, APIs where available, mobile emulators, or other application-specific extraction methods.

Unlike traditional website scraping, mobile app data may be presented through an application interface rather than a standard web page. Some apps may also use technologies such as certificate pinning, authentication, rate limits, or other technical controls. Because of this, the collection method needs to be selected according to the individual app and the type of data being requested.

For businesses, Mobile App Data Extraction can provide visibility into information such as app rankings, pricing, reviews, product catalogues, availability, version changes, and other publicly accessible app data.

Key Features

Key Features of
Mobile App Data Extraction

Mobile applications and app stores have different structures and technical requirements. Our extraction workflows are therefore designed around the specific platform, app, data fields, and refresh frequency involved in each project.

App Store Data Scraping
App Store Data can provide valuable information about how applications are positioned and updated within Apple's ecosystem. For App Store Data Scraping, we can collect relevant information such as app rankings, categories, descriptions, ratings, reviews, version history, and other available listing data.

The collected information can be refreshed according to the monitoring requirements of the project, helping businesses track changes over time rather than relying on a single snapshot.
Google Play Data Extraction
Google Play Data can be collected to monitor application listings, categories, developer information, ratings, reviews, version details, and other available metadata.

Where an appropriate API provides the required information, API-based collection can be used. Where additional publicly available information is required, the extraction approach can be adapted to the structure and access characteristics of the relevant Google Play source.
Android App Scraping & iOS App Coverage
We support both major mobile ecosystems, with the collection approach selected according to the requirements of each platform.

For Android App Scraping, the workflow can focus on Google Play listings or, where applicable, information available within the Android application itself. For iOS, the workflow can cover App Store information and relevant app-level data depending on the project scope. This platform-specific approach helps maintain consistency without assuming that the same extraction method will work across every app.
Mobile App Pricing Data Extraction
App pricing can vary based on region, subscription tier, promotions, in-app purchases, and other factors. Mobile App Pricing Data Extraction can help businesses monitor these changes across selected apps and markets.

Depending on the app and available data, collection can include app prices, subscription plans, in-app purchase information, promotional pricing, and other publicly accessible pricing signals.
Menu & Catalogue Data
Food delivery, Q-commerce, and retail applications often contain product and catalogue information that may differ from a company's website.

Our Mobile App Data Extraction workflows can collect available menu and catalogue information such as products, items, modifiers, prices, images, categories, and availability, depending on the target application. Multi-Country Mobile App Data
Mobile applications may display different catalogues, prices, availability, or app-store information depending on the country or region.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions for
Mobile App Scraping

Q1. What is mobile app scraping and how does it work? +

Mobile app scraping is the process of collecting structured information from mobile applications and app stores. Depending on the target, collections may use app-store sources, APIs, mobile emulators, or other application-specific extraction methods.

Q2. Is mobile app scraping legal and compliant? +

Compliance depends on the target application, type of data, access method, applicable laws, and platform terms. Projects should be reviewed against relevant access restrictions and data-collection requirements before production collection begins.

Q3. Do you scrape both iOS and Android apps? +

Yes. We support data collection across Apple's App Store and Google Play, as well as selected iOS and Android applications, depending on the data required and the technical characteristics of the target app.

Q4. How is mobile app scraping different from regular web scraping? +

Mobile apps can expose data through application interfaces, APIs, app-store listings, or other mobile-specific systems rather than ordinary web pages. They may also use technologies such as certificate pinning or authentication. As a result, the collection method needs to be selected according to the individual application rather than using a standard website scraper for every project.

Q5. Can you extract app pricing and subscription information? +

Yes, where the relevant pricing, subscription, or in-app purchase information is publicly accessible and technically available for collection. The exact fields depend on the application and the data source.

Q6. What format is the data delivered in? +

Validated mobile app data can be delivered in formats such as CSV, JSON, Excel, database-ready output, or through an API, depending on your integration requirements.

Q7. How long does it take to build a custom mobile app data extraction pipeline? +

The timeline depends on the number of target applications, platforms, data fields, markets, collection frequency, and technical requirements. A focused app-store project may require less development than a broader project involving multiple applications and in-app data sources.
