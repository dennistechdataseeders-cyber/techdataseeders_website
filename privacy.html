Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

TechDataSeeders is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website or services. We only extract publicly available data and never scrape personal or identity-related information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you interact with our website or engage our services:

  • Contact information — name, email address, company name, and phone number provided through inquiry forms or direct communication.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, referral source, browser type, and IP address collected automatically via analytics tools.
  • Communication records — emails, messages, or project briefs you share with us during the course of a business engagement.

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial details, government IDs, or health information through our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the services you have requested.
  • Send service updates, project status reports, or relevant communications.
  • Improve our website experience and service quality.
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data We Extract (Our Core Service)

TechDataSeeders provides web scraping and data extraction services. In the course of delivering these services:

  • We only extract publicly available information from websites, apps, and online platforms.
  • We do not scrape, collect, or process personal data, identity-related information, or private records.
  • All data pipelines are designed in compliance with the source website's publicly stated terms of service and applicable data laws.
  • Extracted datasets are delivered exclusively to the client who commissioned the extraction.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic website functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., Google Analytics). Data is anonymised and aggregated.

You can disable cookies via your browser settings at any time. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use the website.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the information we hold, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS) across all website communications.
  • Access controls limiting data to authorised team members only.
  • Secure, access-controlled storage environments for client deliverables.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we take all reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party tools such as analytics platforms, form services, or communication tools. These providers have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies:

  • Google Analytics — for website usage insights.
  • Email service providers — for business communication.

We do not allow third-party vendors to use your data for any purpose beyond the service they provide to us.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Client project data is retained for the duration of the engagement and for a reasonable period thereafter for support and record-keeping purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access the information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion of your data.
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ceo@techdataseeders.in.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any revisions. Continued use of our website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us
