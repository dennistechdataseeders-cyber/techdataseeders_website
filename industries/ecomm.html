Catalog & Inventory Monitoring Services

How Businesses Are Using Catalog & Inventory Data to Reduce Costs and Capture Demand

Maintaining an accurate, up-to-date product catalog across multiple marketplaces and distribution channels is a major operational challenge. Discrepancies in product titles, missing attributes, incorrect images, or outdated inventory levels directly lead to lost sales, returns, and customer dissatisfaction. We help businesses monitor, validate, and synchronize catalog and inventory data at scale using automated data collection pipelines.

Our catalog monitoring solutions extract structured product data including SKU attributes, pricing, availability status, variants, and descriptions from your own systems and competitor platforms. We flag missing fields, duplicate listings, attribute mismatches, and non-compliant content before they impact discoverability. On the inventory side, we track stock levels across warehouses, retail stores, and marketplace fulfillment centers. Real-time alerts notify you when a high-demand SKU falls below safety stock thresholds or when a competitor's product shows sudden unavailability, giving you the chance to adjust ordering or capture displaced demand.

We build custom monitoring pipelines tailored to your product catalog size, channel mix (Shopify, Magento, Amazon, eBay, etc.), and monitoring frequency from hourly to daily. Delivery options include anomaly dashboards, automated catalog audit reports, and API webhooks for instant inventory alerts. With our data, retailers reduce listing errors by 70%, prevent revenue loss from unexpected stockouts, and maintain consistent catalog quality across every sales touchpoint.

Consumer & Market Insights

How Businesses Are Using Consumer Data to Drive Smarter Strategies

Consumer expectations evolve rapidly, and businesses that rely on outdated or surface-level insights fall behind. To stay relevant, companies need real-time visibility into what customers are searching for, buying, reviewing, and abandoning across multiple platforms. We help businesses collect and analyze consumer-generated data from e-commerce reviews, social media conversations, forum discussions, Q&A sections, and product comparison sites. This unstructured data, when processed with AI-driven sentiment analysis and trend detection, transforms into actionable market intelligence.

Our consumer insights solutions track shifting preferences by product category, brand perception over time, feature-level feedback (e.g., "battery life" or "fit"), and demographic-based buying patterns. For example, we can identify rising demand for sustainable packaging in your category weeks before competitors react, or detect negative sentiment spikes around a specific product feature that requires immediate attention. We also benchmark your brand against competitors on factors like price perception, customer service ratings, and delivery experience giving you a clear roadmap for improvement.

We build custom insight pipelines based on your industry, target audience, competitor set, and key attributes you care about. Delivery options include sentiment dashboards, trend reports, review aggregation with AI summaries, and automated alerts for unusual consumer behavior shifts. With our data, marketing teams launch campaigns rooted in real demand, product teams prioritize features customers actually want, and executives track brand health with confidence.

Geography & Market expansion

How Businesses Are Using Geographic Data to Expand Smarter and Faster

Expanding into new cities, states, or countries requires more than instinct it requires hard evidence of demand, competition, pricing dynamics, and local consumer behavior. Businesses that skip data-driven validation often face mismatched product assortments, unexpected pricing pressure, or overlooked local competitors. We help companies collect and analyze location-specific data from region-targeted e-commerce sites, local marketplace platforms, distribution partner portals, regional pricing indexes, and location-based search trends.

Our market expansion solutions track product availability and pricing by geography, competitor presence and share across regions, local promotion patterns, and demand signals from search and social data. For example, we can identify that a product category is underserved in a specific metro area, determine the optimal entry price point based on local competitor benchmarks, or flag that a regional player is aggressively discounting to block new entrants. We also monitor logistics indicators delivery timelines, shipping costs, and stock availability by pin code to help you assess operational feasibility before committing resources.

We build custom geographic intelligence pipelines based on your target expansion zones, product categories, competitor set, and data sources relevant to each region (local marketplaces, aggregators, retailer sites). Delivery options include regional heatmaps, market readiness scorecards, competitor density reports, and API feeds that integrate with your site selection or store planning tools. With our data, expansion teams prioritize high-opportunity regions, reduce costly launch mistakes, and enter new markets with confidence backed by local intelligence.

Competitor Price Monitoring Services

How Businesses Are Using Competitor Price Data to Win on Price Without Sacrificing Profit

Price is often the fastest-changing variable in e-commerce, yet many businesses still monitor competitors manually leading to slow responses, reactive discounting, and lost sales. Real-time competitor price monitoring is no longer optional; it's essential for maintaining search ranking, buy box ownership, and customer trust. We help companies collect and analyze structured pricing data from competitor websites, marketplaces, distributor portals, and comparison shopping engines at scale from hourly to daily refreshes.

Our competitor price monitoring solutions track list prices, sale prices, bundle pricing, per-unit pricing, dynamic surge pricing, shipping costs, and membership-discounted rates. We also monitor historical price trends, repricing velocity, and price positioning relative to category averages. For example, we can detect that a competitor drops price every Tuesday on a specific SKU, identify that your price is 8% above the market leader for a high-volume product, or flag sudden, aggressive discounting aimed at stealing your share before a holiday weekend. We also monitor buy box ownership and rotation patterns on marketplaces like Amazon to help you optimize for visibility.

We build custom price monitoring pipelines based on your SKU list, competitor set, target geographies, preferred refresh frequency (hourly, daily, weekly), and alert thresholds (e.g., "notify when any competitor price drops below X"). Delivery options include price dashboards with trend lines, competitive positioning matrices, automated email/Slack alerts for price changes, and API feeds that plug directly into your dynamic repricing engine or ERP system. With our data, e-commerce teams respond to price moves in minutes, category managers defend margins with surgical precision, and executives gain full visibility into competitive price pressure across every product line.

Rating & Review extraction

How Businesses Are Using Review Data to Improve Products and Customer Experience

Customer reviews are one of the richest sources of unfiltered feedback, yet most businesses only glance at star ratings or read a handful of recent comments. Buried inside thousands of reviews are specific insights feature requests, recurring complaints, unexpected use cases, and direct comparisons with competitors. We help businesses extract and structure review data from e-commerce platforms (Amazon, Walmart, Flipkart), app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store), travel sites (Tripadvisor, Booking.com), restaurant platforms (Yelp, Zomato), and product forums at scale.

Our rating and review extraction solutions capture reviewer names, star ratings, review text, verified purchase badges, helpfulness votes, images/videos attached to reviews, and seller responses. We then apply AI-driven sentiment analysis to break down feedback by specific attributes price, quality, durability, customer service, packaging, shipping speed, size/fit, and more. For example, we can identify that 68% of 3-star reviews mention "battery life" as the primary issue, detect that competitors receive better feedback on "ease of assembly," or surface that customers are using your kitchen gadget for an entirely unintended purpose revealing a new market opportunity.

We build custom review extraction pipelines based on your products, competitor ASINs or product URLs, target platforms, languages, and review attributes you care about. Delivery options include sentiment dashboards with attribute breakdowns, review summaries with AI-generated insights, competitive comparison matrices, and API feeds that push structured review data into your product management or customer insights platform. With our data, product teams fix what customers actually complain about, marketing teams highlight strengths backed by real quotes, and customer experience teams track satisfaction trends month over month.

Sentiment Analysis from Social Media Services

How Businesses Are Using Social Sentiment Data to Connect with Customers Authentically

Social media is where customers speak most freely celebrating wins, venting frustrations, comparing brands, and influencing others. Yet without systematic analysis, these conversations remain noise instead of intelligence. Businesses that ignore social sentiment miss early warning signs of PR crises, fail to recognize sudden shifts in brand love, and lose opportunities to engage when customers are most receptive. We help companies collect and analyze unstructured social data from Twitter/X, Reddit, Facebook, Instagram comments, LinkedIn, TikTok captions, YouTube comments, and niche forums transforming casual posts into structured sentiment insights.

Our sentiment analysis solutions go beyond simple positive/negative classification. We detect nuanced emotions frustration, disappointment, delight, confusion, trust, skepticism and attribute them to specific topics: product features, customer service interactions, delivery experiences, pricing reactions, ad campaigns, or competitor comparisons. For example, we can flag that negative sentiment around your "checkout process" spiked 400% in 24 hours due to a technical glitch, detect that a competitor's new launch is generating "confusion" rather than "excitement," or identify that a specific influencer's mention shifted brand sentiment from neutral to highly positive across a target demographic. We also track sentiment velocity (how fast sentiment is changing), share of voice by sentiment category, and emotion trends by geography or time of day.

We build custom sentiment pipelines based on your brand name, product lines, competitor handles, relevant keywords, target platforms, languages, and demographic filters (region, age indicators from profiles). Delivery options include sentiment dashboards with emotion breakdowns, real-time alerting for sentiment spikes, trend comparison charts across competitors, and API feeds into your social listening or customer experience management systems. With our data, brand teams catch reputation fires before they spread, marketing teams measure campaign emotional resonance, and product teams hear what customers actually feel not just what they click.
